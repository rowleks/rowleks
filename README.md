# Hi, I'm Rowland

I'm a full-stack developer who cares deeply about writing clean, maintainable code, building apps that genuinely solve user problems, and continuously improving my knowledge and skills.

I am studying for a B.S. in Software Development at Brigham Young University-Idaho. I also hold a B.Ed. in Mathematics Education from Kogi State University. Outside of programming, I enjoy reading, writing, chess, football, and puzzles.

I write articles on Medium and Substack, and I'm currently building [Vetdraft](https://vetdraft-orcin.vercel.app/), an AI-powered writing app that helps writers draft, revise, and publish better work.
